# A pattern block's schedule switches patterns on the clock

A pattern block may replace its `pattern:` with `schedule:`, a list of named entries, each a pattern over the block's pools that claims local hours, and optionally weekdays, in the station `tz`. One entry with neither hours nor weekdays fills every hour the others leave unclaimed, and two entries claiming the same hour fail at load. The core walker runs the entry active at its running clock — the generation's `from` plus the runtime laid so far — and switches only between visits, so a visit crossing a boundary airs in full and the boundary drifts. Each entry keeps its own cycle and step in the `.resume` sidecar, so an entry returning the next day continues its pattern where it stopped, and its pools continue their series as a plain pattern block's do. A scheduled block is its channel's only block.
