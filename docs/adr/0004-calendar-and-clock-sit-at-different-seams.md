# Calendar conditioning filters blocks; clock conditioning is a sequencer plugin

A block can declare the calendar dates it airs on, evaluated at channel resolution (`resolve_channel`) before blocks concatenate into one flat item list — a non-matching dated block is dropped there. A block cannot declare a time of day; a channel that wants dayparting names a sequencer plugin (#169) and the script places its pools against the clock, since wall-clock position is resolved only later, in `items_covering`. Cascade priority among dated blocks is the order they are written in `rule.blocks` — there is no `priority:` field. A channel with dated blocks must also declare at least one undated block, which airs unconditionally as the default; a channel lacking one fails at load. A sequencer prefers items that fit the time remaining before its next daypart boundary and lets the boundary drift rather than truncating an item or leaving dead air.
