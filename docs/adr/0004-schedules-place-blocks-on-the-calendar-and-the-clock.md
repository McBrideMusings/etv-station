# Schedules place blocks on the calendar and the clock

A channel's `rule.blocks` are its any-time rules. Above them, `rule.schedules` place named `rule.slots` blocks into minute ranges of the local day in the station's time zone, and each schedule may limit itself to `dates:` and `weekdays:`. At any instant the daemon walks the schedules matching that day from highest to lowest and airs the first one with a range covering that instant; when none has one, the any-time rules air. A schedule with `dates:` outranks one with `weekdays:`, which outranks one with neither, and written order breaks ties. A generation stops laying items at the next instant the airing block changes, and what is airing then — the item, or a pattern block's cycle in progress — finishes first, so a boundary lands late by that item's overrun rather than cutting an item; a block with `align:` fills that overrun with bumpers (docs/schema.md, Align and fill) so the next programme starts on the grid line; no block on such a channel may author `cycles`. Each slot keeps its own resume state.
